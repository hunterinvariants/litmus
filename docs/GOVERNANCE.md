# Benchmark governance

Every case addition or label change must include provenance, an exact upstream
commit for external code, a runnable proof or documented external-corpus basis,
and review of its effect on every committed scorecard.

Tool maintainers may contribute adapters, but may not change ground truth solely
to improve their score. Conflicts of interest must be disclosed in the case,
adapter, evidence report, and release notes.

The Aegis-7702 adapter and case005 are maintained by the same owner as the tool.
They demonstrate reproducible integration, not independent endorsement.
